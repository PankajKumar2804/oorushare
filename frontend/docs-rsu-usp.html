RSU + USP — Revenue & Cashflow in the Gap

How the Last 10 Metres market gap is the engine of OoruShare's entire business model · Built by Pankajkr Jain · Powered by IBM Bob

The Gap That Creates the Opportunity

Every Bengaluru quick-commerce delivery has three stages. The first two are solved. The third is not — and cannot be solved by conventional infrastructure.

Stage 1 — Origin
✓ Solved

Dark Store Loading Bay

Platform's own private property. Managed warehouse dock. Blinkit/Zepto already have this.

Stage 2 — Transit
✓ Solved

En Route Navigation

Google Maps or platform routing. Gets the rider to the customer's street.

Stage 3 — The Gap ← OoruShare
✗ Zero Infrastructure

Customer's Residential Bylane

18–30 ft bylane. No loading zone. No legal stop. Rider parks illegally for 2 minutes. This is the gap.

Why this gap is permanent: BBMP requires a minimum 40 ft road width for formal commercial loading zones. Bengaluru's residential bylanes are 18–30 ft. They cannot be widened. The gap is a hard physical constraint — the only solution is decentralised micro-curb coordination using existing private driveway space.

What Is an RSU?

RSU — Resident Spot Unit
The atomised, monetisable unit of idle residential driveway capacity that fills the gap.

One RSU = one verified residential driveway: geotagged (WGS-84 GPS), RWA-approved, gate width ≥ 2.5 m, made available as a legal 2-minute loading bay during work hours. It is the supply-side atomic asset of the OoruShare network.

RSU Lifecycle — from Idle Driveway to Revenue-Generating Asset

1

Resident Lists the Driveway

60-second web form — address, gate width, available hours. No app install required. Zero friction onboarding.

2

RWA Approves the Spot

Human-in-the-loop governance: the society committee verifies address and gate compliance. RSU is activated.

3

RSU Goes Live on the Rider Map

Approved spot appears as a green pin on the rider's spatial radar — bookable in < 1 second via atomic advisory lock.

4

Delivery Rider Books & Departs

2-minute atomic reservation. Auto-released after departure. No double-booking, no race condition — every dispatched rider generates a micro-transaction fee.

5

Resident Earns Community Credits → OoruShare Earns Revenue

+10 Community Credits per drop = ₹50 maintenance offset per 10 credits = ₹150–₹350 passive income/month for the resident. Each redeemed credit batch triggers a settlement that flows revenue back through the RWA.

Main USP — The Four Unfair Advantages

Core USP in one sentence: OoruShare turns zero-utility idle residential driveways into a city-scale, autonomous, revenue-generating last-mile loading network — without riders needing to download anything, and without residents needing to do anything after a one-time listing.
USP 01 · The B2B Moat

Asymmetric Zero-Friction Rider Onboarding

Riders never download or register on OoruShare. Business Associate Agreements (BAA) with Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy push dispatch instructions automatically via POST /integrations/dispatch. Eliminates cold-start. Locks in demand-side before supply even scales.

USP 02 · The Supply Flywheel

Passive Community Income for Residents

Driveways empty between 9 AM–6 PM generate ₹150–₹350/month automatically off society maintenance dues. No ongoing effort from the resident. Self-reinforcing supply growth — word-of-mouth within RWA networks drives organic RSU listings.

USP 03 · The AI Moat

Agentic Congestion Prevention — Not Reaction

IBM Granite 3.8B time-series forecasting + 4-minute dispatch staggering prevents bylane clustering before it forms. Competitors react to jams with Google Maps reroutes. OoruShare prevents them at the dark store dispatch stage. Structural operational advantage that improves with scale.

USP 04 · The Technical Moat

Sub-Second Race-Condition-Proof Booking

PostgreSQL advisory locks (pg_try_advisory_xact_lock) guarantee atomic spot reservation at peak demand — 2.5M daily drops means hundreds of concurrent booking attempts per bylane. Zero double-bookings = zero resident complaints = supply retention.

Cashflow Generation Loop — The Flywheel

OoruShare's cashflow is a two-sided network flywheel: more RSUs → more dispatch capacity → more BAA value → more platform spending → more credits → more RSU listings. Each of the four flywheel steps maps to a direct cash event.

① Inbound SaaS Revenue

BAA Execution — Platform Licensing

Quick-commerce platforms sign the BAA. Monthly platform fee lands as recurring SaaS MRR. Zero per-transaction overhead — pure subscription margin.

② Transaction Revenue

Dispatch Volume → Per-Drop Fee

Every rider dispatched via POST /integrations/dispatch generates a micro-fee. At 2.5M daily Bengaluru drops even a ₹0.50 per-drop fee = ₹1.25 Cr/day TAM.

③ Community Settlement Spread

Credit Redemption — RWA Float

Residents redeem credits (10 credits = ₹50 maintenance offset). The RWA pays OoruShare a settlement reconciliation fee per credit batch processed — low-volume but zero-CAC.

④ Data Revenue

Civic Data Monetisation — Municipal Licensing

Aggregated congestion telemetry sold to BBMP and BTP as curb-zoning intelligence. Zero marginal cost — data is a byproduct of operations, not a separate workstream.
