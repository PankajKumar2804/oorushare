What OoruShare Is

OoruShare is a four-sided civic marketplace that coordinates residents, gig riders, dark store operators, and RWA committees into a single autonomous curb-sharing economy. The core product is a 2-minute verified residential loading bay operated as a shared civic utility across Bengaluru's high-density bylanes.

The Two-Sided Market

OoruShare solves the marketplace cold-start problem through an asymmetric onboarding architecture — supply and demand are seeded through completely different channels.

Supply Side

Residents → RSU Listings

  • Onboarded via RWA meetings, society gate notices, MyGate / NoBrokerHood
  • 60-second web form — address, gate width, available hours
  • RWA committee approves each listing (human-in-the-loop gate)
  • +10 Community Credits per completed drop
  • 10 credits = ₹50 RWA maintenance deduction
  • ₹150–₹350/month passive income from an idle driveway
Demand Side

Riders & Fleets → BAA Integration

  • Riders never download or register on OoruShare
  • Dark stores sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
  • Shift rosters fed via enterprise webhooks (POST /integrations/dispatch)
  • Curb bay coordinates pushed inside the rider's existing delivery app
  • Zero onboarding friction = zero cold-start drop-off
  • DPDP Act 2023 compliant: rider IDs tokenized

Cashflow Generation Loop — Step by Step

Core insight: OoruShare's primary cashflow comes from B2B, not B2C. Delivery platforms pay for access; residents and riders are value receivers, not payers. Revenue scales with dark store dispatch volume — not individual user acquisition cost.
1

BAA Execution → Anchor License Revenue Starts

A quick-commerce platform (e.g. Blinkit) signs a Business Associate Agreement. Monthly SaaS license fee begins. This is the primary anchor revenue — predictable, recurring, B2B. Contract price negotiated per platform.

2

Live Dispatches Flow In → Per-Drop Micro-Revenue

Dark stores call POST /integrations/dispatch at order checkout. Every successful curb bay allocation charges a micro-fee (e.g. ₹2/drop). Bengaluru's 2.5M drops/day is the total addressable dispatch volume.

3

Resident Credits Earned → Community Value (Cost-of-Supply)

Each drop awards +10 Community Credits = ₹50 maintenance offset to the resident. This is OoruShare's cost of supply — paid via billing offsets, not direct cash, keeping gross margin high.

4

RWA Reconciliation → Secondary Subscription Revenue

RWA committees settle credit redemptions monthly via the RWA Admin Portal. The governance + reconciliation integration service carries a small annual subscription fee per society.

5

Congestion Data Matures → Civic Data Product

After 6–12 months of operation, anonymized curb saturation patterns become a licensable municipal intelligence product for BBMP and BTP — near-100% margin once the data infrastructure is built.

Revenue Activation Roadmap

Phase 0 — Live Now (Prototype)

Full 4-dashboard working prototype with synthetic seed data (24 spots, 18 residents, 20 bookings, 8 redemptions). Credit ledger, RWA governance, agentic dispatch, SSE live radar — all functional. Revenue engine technically validated, awaiting BAA execution.

Phase 1 — Post-Hackathon · BAA Execution

Sign BAA with 1 quick-commerce platform (Blinkit / Zepto). Activate live fleet webhooks. WhatsApp OTP resident login. Per-drop micro-fee revenue begins Day 1 of integration go-live.

Phase 2 — Scale to 5 Corridors · UPI Redemption

Expand to HSR, Indiranagar, Koramangala, Whitefield, JP Nagar. Direct UPI credit payouts to residents and society maintenance funds. Onboard 2nd and 3rd platforms under BAA framework.

Phase 3 — Citywide · Civic Data Monetization

RWA annual subscription tiers launched. Anonymized congestion heatmap licensed to BBMP and BTP. 200+ corridor coverage. OoruShare becomes Bengaluru's curb intelligence layer.

Why This Business Model Is Defensible

Demand-Side Moat

BAA Lock-In

Once Blinkit's dispatch pipeline integrates OoruShare's API, switching cost = re-engineering dispatch infrastructure. Network stickiness compounds with every routed delivery.

Supply-Side Moat

Community Loyalty

Residents earning ₹150–₹350/month passively will not remove their RSU listing. RWA endorsement creates social trust that is near-impossible to displace competitively.

Regulatory Moat

DPDP + RWA Governance

DPDP Act 2023 compliance, BAA legal framework, and RWA democratic approval gate make OoruShare the trusted, compliant default for civic institutions and municipal partners.

AI Moat

Proprietary Congestion Intelligence

IBM Granite 3.8B trained on OoruShare's own bylane saturation data creates proprietary dispatch intelligence no competitor can replicate without the same network history.
