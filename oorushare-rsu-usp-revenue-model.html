What Is an RSU?

RSU — Resident Spot Unit
The atomised, monetisable unit of idle residential driveway capacity

An RSU is a single private driveway registered by a Bengaluru resident on OoruShare — verified by the local RWA, geotagged (WGS-84 GPS), and made available as a legal 2-minute loading bay during standard work hours (9 AM – 6 PM). It is the core supply-side asset of the entire platform.

RSU Lifecycle

1

Resident Lists the Driveway

60-second web form: address, gate width (min 2.5 m), available slot hours. No app install required.

2

RWA Approves the Spot

Human-in-the-loop governance: the RWA committee verifies address legitimacy and gate compliance. Spot is inactive until approval.

3

RSU Goes Live on the Radar

Approved spot appears on the rider's ESRI spatial radar as an available green pin — bookable in < 1 second via PostgreSQL advisory lock.

4

Delivery Rider Books & Departs

2-minute atomic reservation. Auto-released after departure or 2-minute window. No double-booking, no race conditions.

5

Resident Earns Credits

+10 Community Credits automatically awarded per completed drop. Credits → ₹50 RWA maintenance deduction per 10 credits = ₹150–₹350/month passive income for the resident.

Main USP — The Unfair Advantages

Core USP: OoruShare turns zero-utility idle residential driveways into a city-scale, autonomous, self-balancing loading bay network — without requiring delivery riders to install a single new app.
USP 01

Asymmetric Zero-Friction Onboarding

Riders never download or register on OoruShare. B2B Business Associate Agreements (BAA) with Blinkit, Zepto & Swiggy stream live shift rosters via enterprise webhooks directly into existing rider delivery apps. Eliminates the cold-start problem.

USP 02

Passive Community Income for Residents

Driveways sitting empty between 9 AM–6 PM generate ₹150–₹350/month automatically off society maintenance dues. The only civic platform that turns an idle driveway into a financial asset with zero daily effort.

USP 03

Agentic Congestion Prevention (Not Reaction)

IBM Granite 3.8B time-series forecasting + 4-minute dispatch staggering stops bylane clustering before riders leave the dark store. The only platform that fixes gridlock upstream at dispatch, not downstream at the curb.

USP 04

Sub-Second Race-Condition-Proof Booking

PostgreSQL advisory locks (pg_try_advisory_xact_lock) guarantee atomic spot reservation during evening surge peaks — no double-bookings, no phantom slots, no stale radar pins.

Cashflow Generation Loop

OoruShare's cashflow is driven by a two-sided network flywheel: more RSUs → more dispatch integrations → more platform revenue → more credits earned → more residents list RSUs.

①

BAA Execution — Inbound SaaS Revenue

Quick-commerce platforms (Blinkit/Zepto/Swiggy) sign a Business Associate Agreement. Monthly platform licensing fee + per-drop dispatch fee begins flowing in from day one of integration.

②

Dispatch Volume → Per-Drop Revenue

Every delivery rider dispatch routed through POST /integrations/dispatch generates a micro-fee. Bengaluru alone processes 2.5M drops/day — even 0.1% capture × ₹2/drop = ₹5,000/day from a single corridor cluster.

③

Credit Redemption Spread — Community Offset

Residents redeem credits (10 credits = ₹50 maintenance offset). The RWA pays OoruShare the maintenance-billing integration fee. Net: residents gain ₹150–₹350/month, OoruShare earns a small reconciliation margin per society.

④

Civic Data Monetization — Municipal Licensing

Aggregated, anonymized congestion telemetry sold to BBMP and BTP as a curb-zoning intelligence feed. Zero PII — only geospatial saturation data. One-time setup + annual license fee per municipal ward.
